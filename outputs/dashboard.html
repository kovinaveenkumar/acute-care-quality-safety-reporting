2. What needs the committee's attention

High Fall events on MSW well above that unit's own baseline · Patient Safety

Evidence: 2026-03 to 2026-05: 31 events vs 9.1 expected (12.5 vs 3.7 per 1,000 patient days; 3.4x; Poisson p=1e-08).

Suggested action: Review the event narratives for the window, check for a process or equipment change at that time, and report back to the committee with an action plan.

High Sepsis bundle compliance dipped well below baseline · Core Measures

Evidence: Lowest month 2026-01: 45% vs a 67% baseline. In 2025-12 to 2026-02 the weakest element was repeat lactate within 6h (66% vs 86% at baseline).

Suggested action: Check whether order sets or documentation changed in that window; re-educate on the failing element.

Medium Medication event rate above the upper control limit · Patient Safety

Evidence: 2026-01: 9.4 per 1,000 patient days vs centre line 4.2 (UCL 7.5).

Suggested action: Compare with the timing of system changes (e.g., EHR updates) and sample events for ordering/administration steps.

Medium VTE prophylaxis gap: MSW Weekend · Core Measures

Evidence: MSW Weekend: 79.3% (n=725) vs 92.9% for all other unit/day combinations (chi-square p=1e-38).

Suggested action: Review weekend admission order-set use and pharmacy verification on that unit.

Medium 3 measure(s) are Red in the latest quarter · Scorecard

Evidence: CLABSI per 1,000 central-line days 2.1 (target 1); CAUTI per 1,000 catheter days 1.5 (target 1.3); 30-day readmission, heart failure (%) 22.3 (target 20). Small denominators make single-quarter infection rates volatile; interpret with the control charts.

Suggested action: Review the red measures with the owning department and confirm whether each is a real change or normal variation.

Medium Heart-failure 30-day readmissions peaked · Outcomes

Evidence: 2026 Q1: 30.2% (target 20%). Latest quarter 22.3%.

Suggested action: Review post-discharge follow-up scheduling and discharge education for HF patients discharged in the peak quarter.

Medium ED throughput signals during the winter surge · Emergency Department

Evidence: Out-of-control points in 2025-10 to 2026-03. ED median admit-decision-to-departure (min): peak 197.3; ED median arrival-to-departure, discharged (min): peak 186.7; ED left without being seen (%): peak 4.0.

Suggested action: Review surge staffing and inpatient bed-flow plans before next winter.

Medium Abstraction backlog · Quality Operations

Evidence: On-time completion fell to 38% (centre line 90%) for cases selected 2026-02 to 2026-04.

Suggested action: Rebalance case assignment across abstractors; protect time before quarterly submission deadlines.

Medium Public-reporting submission submitted late: SEP1 2025Q4 · Quality Operations

Evidence: Due 2026-04-30; submitted 2026-05-03.

Suggested action: Add a 30-day reminder to the abstraction calendar and name a backup submitter.
